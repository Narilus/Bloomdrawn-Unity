# Bloom Identity Pass - Refraction, Domains, and Art Guidance

This concept note supports `docs/DESIGN.md` by collecting richer guidance for the Bloom identity pass. `docs/DESIGN.md` remains the source of truth; this file is a planning and content-direction companion for future enemy, Symptom, Labyrinth, character, event, and art tasks.

## Core Thesis

The Bloom is a generative cosmic pressure or event. It does not replace the four Domains and is not a fifth Domain. It refracts through what already exists: bodies, belief, souls, ecosystems, memories, architecture, water, hidden wounds, rituals, and desires.

The Bloom is frightening because it is not only destructive. It can heal, beautify, preserve, adapt, empower, comfort, or reveal. The cost is that something else grows with the benefit.

Useful short statements:

- The Bloom is the pressure; the Domains are how reality responds.
- The Bloom refracts rather than simply infects.
- The Bloom must remain beautiful enough that accepting it can seem reasonable.
- Transformation is not always moral failure, but survival may still become replacement.

## Refraction Lexicon

Preferred verbs and nouns:

- bloom;
- refract;
- root;
- flower;
- fruit;
- seed;
- graft;
- unfold;
- prune;
- cultivate;
- preserve;
- internalize.

Use "lifecycle" or "growth stage" as the umbrella term. Avoid using "corruption" as the default umbrella, because the Bloom is not only moral decay or disease.

## Domain Refraction

### Flesh

Flesh is the Domain of visible Bloom-embrace. Characters in this Domain have often learned to love, aestheticize, or evangelize the beauty and power that comes with the Bloom's temptation.

Art and writing center:

- flowers, petals, blooms, blood, flesh, mouths, grafts, sutures, warmth;
- propagation, reproduction, caretaking, gardening, surgical devotion;
- Embryo as gameplay expression of growth, gestation, and spreading the gift;
- visibly altered silhouettes that remain desirable and readable.

Avoid making every Flesh character a grotesque mass. The gacha appeal comes from beauty, confidence, intimacy, care, or glamour colliding with body horror.

### Abyss

Abyss is not primarily a sea-monster Domain. Before the Bloom, many Abyss-aligned people were cultists, fanatics, mystics, or devotees of an old god. The Bloom refracts their devotion and imagined divinity into tangible form.

Art and writing center:

- old-god worship, ritual dress, fanatic tenderness, hymns, relics, pressure, drowning, impossible depth;
- tentacles as belief made physical rather than generic aquatic anatomy;
- lure-lights, drowned chapels, devotional markings, pressure-glass, ceremonial veils;
- bodily transformation echoing the imagined god, without proving whether that god was real before the Bloom.

The old god's status should stay ambiguous. The worshippers may have been correct, deluded, or made correct by the Bloom.

### Spirit

Spirit is the Domain of soul continuity through refracted bodies. It includes humanoid-first hybrids and chimera whose bodies may be fused, mixed, copied, or altered, while Essence, memory, oath, or soul remains the unifying identity.

Art and writing center:

- humanoid-first designs with readable character appeal;
- chimera accents, fused traits, spirit companions, reflected bodies, veils, glass, halos, ritual posture;
- Essence as preparation, channeling, memory, and spiritual cohesion;
- fear or acceptance of being more than one body while still one self.

Spirit can include non-humanoid beings, but launch and gacha-facing character art should usually preserve readable humanoid appeal.

### Void

Void is the wildcard and internalized Domain. It catches characters whose transformation does not clearly belong to Flesh, Abyss, or Spirit, or who have contained, negotiated, hidden, or internalized their change.

Art and writing center:

- neutral or domain-less presentation;
- thresholds, bargains, rules, restraint, silence, sealed changes, strange calm;
- subtle anomalies in timing, gaze, reflection, shadow, costume edges, or posture;
- power that manipulates cost, intent, timing, delay, debt, and exceptions.

Avoid making Void primarily "negative-space people." Absence can be one motif, but the art direction should remain character-designable.

## Bloom Lifecycle Stages

These stages are canon language for content guidance, not mandatory mechanics.

### Dormant

The Bloom is present but easily mistaken for ordinary beauty.

Signs: unusual pastel coloration, radial markings, growths in wrong materials, flowers turning toward living creatures, sweet scent, soft repetition.

### Seeded

The host has begun changing while mostly retaining its original form and behaviour.

Signs: scars budding, ceramic with a pulse, roots following veins, devotional marks moving when unobserved, a remembered voice repeating in glass.

### Rooted

The Bloom has integrated into the host's systems and serves a function.

Functions: feeding, defense, movement, memory storage, mimicry, communication, regeneration, worship, concealment.

### Flowering

The host openly expresses Domain-shaped transformation.

The host may still retain personality, affection, routine, speech, or beauty, creating tension between recognizable identity and transformed purpose.

### Fruiting

The host or place becomes a reproductive structure, environmental hazard, elite organism, boss state, or source of further spread.

Fruiting can be subtle: copied voices, detachable glass petals, walking buds, drifting seeds, lure-lights, ritual echoes, or sealed internal changes reaching outward.

## Botanical and Anatomical Fusion

Preferred visual solutions:

- petals continuous with skin, membrane, cloth, or scale;
- stems following tendons, ribs, hair, or ritual cords;
- veins branching into leaf venation;
- roots functioning as nerves, digestive structures, sensory organs, or devotional bindings;
- stamens resembling cilia, fingers, teeth, antennae, lashes, or prayer tools;
- pollen acting as spores, memories, voices, eggs, static, light, or longing;
- fruiting structures storing blood, water, emotion, doctrine, or absence.

Avoid:

- ordinary flowers pasted onto otherwise conventional monsters;
- all Domains becoming floral Flesh variants;
- pastel recolours that do not alter material, motion, anatomy, behaviour, or silhouette;
- replacing character appeal with unreadable abstraction.

## Labyrinth Growth Geometry

The Labyrinth may be read as land and memory reorganized by the Bloom.

Useful motif meanings:

- Main Spine: stem, root, vein, pilgrimage route, or central nerve.
- Side Loop: petal, branching tissue, devotional circuit, or tempting growth.
- Fragile Path: brittle stalk, new connective tissue, collapsing bridge, or unstable bargain.
- Corrupted Edge: root, capillary, pressure current, ritual trace, or spreading internal change.
- Symptom Gate: concentrated contact with the Bloom.
- Shop Loop: cultivation, trade, pruning, domestication, or opportunistic harvesting.
- Boss Region: flower heart, seed chamber, fruiting body, old-god focus, chimera nest, or sealed internal bloom.

Do not require every map to look literally floral. Repetition, branching, symmetry, route costs, and one-time growth can carry the concept.

## Character Philosophy Notes

Launch characters should share the question of transformation without sharing the same answer.

- Mara: growth as care, surgery, management, and necessary tenderness.
- Venelis: growth as being seen, opened, adored, and spread.
- Thalassa: growth as devotion under pressure and survival through depth.
- Nyxalia: growth as performance, lure, beauty, and predatory worship.
- Sephira: growth as preservation, hymn, memory, and spiritual meaning.
- Kibane: growth as a soul binding multiple inherited shapes into one deliberate self.
- Azael: growth as negotiation with boundaries and hidden internal change.
- Mira Nox: growth as restraint, redirection, and use of the uncategorized to prevent worse outcomes.

## Internal Enemy Relationship Buckets

These labels are brainstorming tools, not canon taxonomy unless a later decision promotes them.

- Bloomtouched: ordinary creature, object, or place showing early signs.
- Bloomgrown: organism or structure produced almost entirely by the Bloom.
- Bloombound: host in stable or unstable symbiosis.
- Bloomcultivated: intentionally bred, trained, pruned, worshipped, or weaponized.
- Bloomhollow: structure or body still imitating a former host after identity is gone.
- Bloomresistant: subject damaged or distorted by actively rejecting growth.

Use these buckets to vary enemies. Do not expose all of them as player-facing categories by default.


## Unity 2D Presentation Notes

These notes translate the identity guidance into the Unity production workflow without changing the art direction.

- AI-generated art is permitted for reference, prototype, production, and final use. A generated asset can be release-quality after human review; generation method is provenance, not placeholder status.
- Character combat art should normally be produced as an independent transparent full-body actor asset rather than baked into a party composite. When useful, secondary-motion elements such as hair, cloth, tentacles, petals, ornaments, or spirit companions may be delivered as separable layers, but skeletal rigging is optional rather than mandatory.
- Independently targetable enemies should likewise have their own actor art/target footprint. Decorative boss limbs, environmental blooms, and background creatures may be separate non-targetable layers.
- Rich background/environment images are encouraged, but critical target silhouettes, HP/intents, Domain counters, and card-play indicators must remain readable over them.
- Art generation should consider gameplay scale from the start: clean silhouette, transparent edge quality, useful negative space around weapons/tentacles, and enough resolution for modest camera/transform motion without visible degradation.
- Unity animation may be achieved through transform motion, Animator clips, frame animation, SpriteSkin/2D Animation, shader/VFX motion, or combinations thereof. The project should not force a character into a rigging workflow when simple layered 2D motion better preserves the illustration.
- Domain identity should survive both full-motion and reduced-motion modes; an important Flesh/Abyss/Spirit/Void tell cannot exist only in a subtle animation.

## Writing Restraint

The Bloom should not be explained all the way down. Characters may call it a god, infection, ecology, reproductive event, judgement, miracle, weapon, weather, or invitation. Several interpretations may be partially true.

Write scenes around what changed, what the change offered, what was lost, and what someone chooses next.
